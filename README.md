The NSCLC workflow links RNA, copy-number, reported-variant and clinical/specimen metadata for 507 LUAD and 481 LUSC tumors, with 25 provisional RNA programs. An exploratory extension evaluates a fixed panel of 50 published Hallmark programs in three acquired-resistant patients across MCC and melanoma. The six-cell melanoma endpoint and unassessable stricter sensitivity prevent robust MSI nomination. No MSI was nominated, and independent NSCLC validation was not reached. These are exploratory research results, not clinical predictions or causal resistance mechanisms
